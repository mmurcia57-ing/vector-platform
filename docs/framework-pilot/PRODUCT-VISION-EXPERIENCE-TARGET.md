# VECTOR — Product Vision & Experience Target (FR-050 L2)

## Test contract
Minimal intent under test: **build VECTOR as an operational intelligence and performance platform**.

The Framework, not the user, owns discovery of product shape, Knowledge Challenge, competing alternatives and expectation materialization.

## Product Vision

VECTOR is an **Operational Intelligence Workspace**, not a dashboard collection.

Its primary product loop is:

`ATTEND → ORIENT → INVESTIGATE → DECIDE → ACT → VERIFY`

The workspace lets an operations/technology decision-maker start from an evidence-backed operational signal, preserve service/area/time context while moving through topology and temporal evidence, distinguish observed facts from derived findings and hypotheses, govern a commitment/action, and later verify whether the operational condition changed.

### Product identity
- Spatial + temporal operational workspace.
- Evidence-first investigation.
- Context continuity across portfolio, service, investigation, action and outcome.
- Progressive disclosure instead of card proliferation.
- System/process intelligence, not individual scoring.
- Correlation remains distinct from causality.
- Execution remains distinct from verified outcome.

### Explicit non-goals
- KPI-card dashboard as the primary experience.
- Decorative topology.
- Fake live telemetry or invented causal arrows.
- Individual productivity ranking.
- AI narrative presented as fact.
- One giant graph containing catalog, runtime, temporal and memory semantics without provenance.

## Competing Experience Targets

### A — Operational Field
**Grammar:** spatial-first.

The browser is primarily an operational field, not a page. Services/domains occupy a navigable topology field. Attention signals appear as evidence-backed overlays. A temporal rail is permanently coordinated with selected topology context. Selecting a service changes the evidence/context inspector rather than navigating to another dashboard.

Strength: strongest material departure from dashboard grammar; best topology/context continuity.
Risk: can become visual spectacle/hairball.
Experiment: bounded topology + focus/expand + temporal synchronization + accessible table fallback.

### B — Investigation Timeline
**Grammar:** temporal-first.

The main surface is an operational event spine: condition changes, incidents, monitoring events, changes, commitments/actions and outcomes. Topology appears contextually for the selected interval/event.

Strength: strong evidence-first/change-investigation fit.
Risk: weaker portfolio orientation and spatial dependency comprehension.
Experiment: J02 degradation/change and anti-causality scenario.

### C — Dual Canvas
**Grammar:** coordinated spatial + temporal.

Two persistent, synchronized canvases:
1. **Operational Field** for portfolio/service topology and attention.
2. **Evidence Spine** for temporal sequence, changes, incidents, actions and outcomes.

A context inspector progressively discloses provenance, limitations, deterministic findings and hypotheses. There is no route change between “dashboard pages”; context focus changes the workspace.

Strength: balances command breadth with investigation depth and preserves context.
Risk: density/complexity; requires disciplined progressive disclosure.
Experiment: J01 persistent risk, J02 change association/anti-causality, shared dependency, stale/partial evidence, healthy negative control.

## Candidate decision for materialization

**C — Dual Canvas** is selected as the first Experience Target experiment, not yet as final design.

Reason:
- materially different from rejected card/dashboard baseline;
- synthesizes spatial hypotheses from ATSMATRIX/Smartscape/ServiceRadar with temporal-history implications from Toise;
- aligns with evidence-first investigation;
- supports command → investigation → action/outcome without separate dashboard pages;
- can degrade to accessible lists/tables and bounded focus.

## Experience Target contract

The materialized target MUST visibly demonstrate:
1. browser-dominant operational field rather than stacked KPI cards;
2. persistent context: selected area/service/time;
3. topology nodes + typed relationships with bounded focus;
4. synchronized temporal/event spine;
5. evidence inspector with provenance/freshness/limitations;
6. facts vs derived findings vs hypothesis/decision distinction;
7. transition from finding → commitment/action → outcome verification without losing context;
8. healthy/unknown/stale/partial states;
9. ES/EN coherent presentation boundary;
10. accessible non-spatial relationship representation.
