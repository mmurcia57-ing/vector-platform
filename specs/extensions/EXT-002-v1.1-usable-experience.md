# VECTOR — EXT-002 V1.1 Usable Experience

## 1. Metadata and gate

- Status: READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION
- Scope: local VECTOR V1.1 usable experience
- SPEC-BLOCKERS: 0
- Extends: approved V1 and EXT-001 semantics
- Canonical model: unchanged; exactly 17 V1 entities
- External authority: unchanged; OQ-009 remains OPEN

EXT-002 is a controlled experience and hardening extension. It does not
redefine the domain model, add canonical entities, resolve Open Questions, or
authorize Evolution Backlog capabilities.

## 2. Purpose

Turn the functional local VECTOR engine into a usable, navigable, and
presentation-quality V1.1 product experience while preserving deterministic
intelligence, Evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and the approved BFF
architecture boundary.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 Management Experience

The Management Experience answers: “¿Dónde debo intervenir?”

It comprises:

1. Panorama Ejecutivo: attention areas, explainable drivers, reliability and
   risk context, Commitment state, Outcome state, and drill-down to Area
   Intelligence. No universal Area Score or invented Capacity metrics.
2. Area Intelligence: AreaDomain selection followed by services, risks,
   Evidence, Commitments, and Outcomes with retained context.
3. Commitments & Improvements: list, filter, create, and detail over EXT-001,
   including one accountable AreaDomain, optional responsibleParty, due date,
   deterministic overdue state, status, Evidence/source, optional technical
   context, ImprovementAction, OutcomeVerification, and COMPLETED != IMPROVED.

### 3.2 SRE Experience

1. Service Intelligence: a dedicated service view using Service as the
   primary reliability correlation anchor and ConfigurationItem as technical
   or topological context. It exposes available SLO, events, incidents,
   problems, changes/deployments, RiskFindings, Evidence, Commitments, and
   Outcomes.
2. Risk Investigation: a bounded investigative workspace for RiskFinding,
   Evidence, Timeline, Graph, Commitments, ImprovementActions, and
   OutcomeVerification. Graph views are connected, bounded, explainable, and
   projection-only.

### 3.3 Navigation and context

Implement durable navigation and deep-link/context restoration for:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  P[Panorama Ejecutivo] --> A[Area Intelligence]
  A --> C[Commitments & Improvements]
  S[Service Intelligence] --> R[Risk Investigation]
  A --> S
  A --> R
  R --> C
```

Relevant Period, AreaDomain, Service, and RiskFinding context remains
continuous. The interaction hierarchy remains ATTENTION → CONTEXT →
EXPLANATION → EVIDENCE/ACTION/OUTCOME.

### 3.4 Runtime graph

Expose seeded, bounded connected projections through:

`GraphQueryService → BFF → bounded GraphView DTO → SPA`

Neo4j remains a rebuildable projection/read model, never canonical authority.
No new GRC or relationship semantics are introduced.

### 3.5 J02 experience

Materialize the existing contextual flow:

`Change → Deployment → Service → degradation/event → Incident → temporal/contextual correlation → Evidence → RiskFinding → Explanation`

Correlation never implies causation. Missing or uncertain Evidence remains
visible and uncertain.

### 3.6 Performance and hardening

Complete the existing mixed-workload and STRESS characterization using
approved local/synthetic profiles only. Evaluate projection/outbox restart
behavior within the approved SQLite → lightweight outbox → in-process
projector → Neo4j architecture. No broker or alternate architecture is added.

## 4. Non-goals and preserved invariants

EXT-002 does not implement Capacity Intelligence, Delivery Quality/Rework,
Objectives & Outcomes, Decision Management, AI-assisted commitment extraction,
Predictive Commitments, Organizational Friction, broader performance
intelligence, Business/Customer Outcome Intelligence, DRP intelligence,
Engineering Flow/Value/Cost Intelligence, Operating Model Intelligence,
Technology Investment Intelligence, expanded metric catalogs, or business
process semantic extensions.

It preserves exactly 17 canonical entities; Service as the primary technology
correlation anchor; Evidence First; Source Authority and provenance; Identity
!= Correlation; Correlation != Causation; Execution != Outcome; COMPLETED !=
IMPROVED; `RiskFinding → Commitment → ImprovementAction →
OutcomeVerification`; no individual performance scoring; no invented corporate
authority; and SPA → BFF access without direct storage/provider access.

## 5. Data and state behavior

Every experience must preserve available source, authority, provenance,
freshness, confidence, missing context, conflicting claims, and uncertainty.
Partial, stale, unavailable, insufficient, and not-yet-verifiable states are
explicit. A completed action does not assert structural improvement; outcome
verification requires sufficient post-action operational Evidence.

## 6. Visual authority

Repository UX and interaction specifications are normative. Existing design
references may guide visual language only when directly available and
verifiable. No Figma-specific requirement is asserted without access to the
design source. Each experience retains its distinct decision purpose and is
not flattened into a generic dashboard.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

- Management navigation demonstrates Panorama → Area → Commitments.
- SRE navigation demonstrates Service → Risk Investigation.
- Area, Service, RiskFinding, Evidence, Commitment, Action, and Outcome
  context remains continuous through supported navigation and deep links.
- Administrative and technological EXT-001 Commitments are visible with
  approved accountability, due/overdue, provenance, and outcome semantics.
- J01–J04 remain representable and their existing semantic tests continue to
  pass.
- Graph runtime is bounded, connected, contextual, projection-only, and
  truthful about stale/partial/unavailable state.
- J02 displays temporal/contextual association without causal claims.
- Mixed workload and STRESS evidence uses only approved local/synthetic
  profiles.
- Restart behavior is evaluated without changing the approved architecture.
- SPA boundary, Source Authority, Identity, Correlation, Causation, and AI
  non-authority invariants remain intact.

## 8. Quality gate result

The EXT-002 internal specification audit passes: no closed semantic is
redefined, no entity or relationship is added, no corporate fact is invented,
no Evolution candidate is promoted, and the scope is decomposable into the
approved V1.1 task DAG. EXT-002 is READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION.
